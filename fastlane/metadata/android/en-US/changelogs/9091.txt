The download is about 85 MB smaller (#762). The UML kernel and the Go library shipped with their debug information attached, because the build's automatic strip pass only covers libraries it compiles itself. Both are now stripped in the scripts that fetch and build them, and since they are stored uncompressed in the APK the saving lands directly on the download. Behaviour is unchanged: the stripped kernel boots a guest and the stripped Go library serves the same features, verified on device.
