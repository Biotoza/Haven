- **Tunnels to hosts on a Tailscale console nameserver connect.** A Tailscale connection to a hostname served by a global or split nameserver set in the Tailscale admin console failed with "no such host" — that nameserver is only reachable through the tailnet, but the lookup used the phone's own DNS. Names are now resolved through the tailnet's resolver first, falling back to the previous lookup, so MagicDNS names, IPs and public hosts are unchanged (#691).
