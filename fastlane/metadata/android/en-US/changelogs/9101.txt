Box-drawing lines, bullets, and status bars in tmux and TUI apps no longer wrap across two rows or eat the following space under Auto+Wide (#749). When the terminal font measured every ambiguous glyph as 1 cell wide, an empty custom-wide table fell back to 2-cell widths and drifted from tmux's 1-cell grid. Multi-row snapshot rebuilds also hold the native lock across the full row batch, and switching tabs keeps the soft keyboard and PTY size steady.
